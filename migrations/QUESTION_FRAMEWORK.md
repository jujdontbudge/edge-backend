## ID Schema
- `{topic_short}_{3-digit-number}` e.g., `prob_001`, `ev_001`, `comb_001`
- Topic shorts: prob, ev, comb, brain, math, stat, seq, game, fermi, mc, disc, calc, la, sp

## Difficulty Levels
1. Warm-up (first phone screen, anyone can solve)
2. Standard phone screen
3. Standard in-person interview
4. Hard interview (separates good from great)
5. Very hard (PhD-level, only top firms)

## Deduplication Rules
- No question appears under two topics
- EV questions must involve computing an expected value or optimal betting decision
- Probability questions focus on P(event) calculations, not EV
- Combinatorics = counting, not probability
- Brain teasers = logic/lateral thinking with no calculation
